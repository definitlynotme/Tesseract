Qt for Python community distribution notice
===========================================

Tesseract uses the community edition of PySide6 and Shiboken6, and the Qt
libraries shipped by those wheels. Tesseract selects the GPL-3.0-only option
for this distribution. The complete GNU GPL version 3 text is distributed
next to this notice as GPL-3.0.txt.

Upstream licensing information:
https://doc.qt.io/qtforpython-6/licenses.html

Upstream source code:
https://code.qt.io/cgit/pyside/pyside-setup.git/
https://code.qt.io/cgit/qt/qtbase.git/
https://code.qt.io/cgit/qt/qtsvg.git/

Only Qt modules and plugins named in the release SBOM may be redistributed in
the Tesseract binary package. The release build rejects unapproved Qt
artifacts, including Qt PDF, Qt Quick/QML, and Qt Virtual Keyboard.
